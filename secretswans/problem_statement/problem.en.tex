\section*{}
The Grand Emperor Cygnus has given the command, the Black Swans of the Derbarl Yerrigan shall wage all-out war against the Mallard Ducks for control of the lake's prime bread-crust foraging zones.
To transmit the strike timing across the river without duck patrols intercepting the message. The swans send their times through a series of squawks (0) and squeeks (1).
Find the minimum number of squawks and squeeks that a swan must make in order to get their message across

\section*{Input}
The input consists of a single line containing a non-empty string $S$ ($1 \le |S| \le 10^5$), representing the secret battle strategy. $S$ contains only lowercase English letters (\texttt{a}-\texttt{z}) and spaces.

\section*{Output}
Output a single integer representing the total bit length of the encoded message.
